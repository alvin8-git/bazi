Author of the bazifor.me guides · builder of the engine · 知识库作者

BaziMaster writes the guides on this site and built the engine behind it. The engine turns a birth moment into a BaZi chart with the traditional solar calendar and true-solar time, reads a traced floor plan into flying-star and Eight House (八宅) maps, and scores Chinese baby names by useful element, 五格 strokes, 三才, meaning and sound. Every line of output names the classical rule it applies, so a reader, or a practitioner, can check it.

The method is deterministic on purpose. The same birth data always gives the same chart; the same floor plan always gives the same stars. Where schools disagree, for example on a character's element or a stroke count, the engine records the choice it made and marks it as disputed rather than hiding it. The flying-star work was checked against a paid professional audit of a real home and matched it star for star.

What this site does not claim

  • It does not predict events. It computes classical structures and states the traditional reading of them.
  • It does not replace a practitioner for decisions with real consequences; it gives you the working to bring to one.
  • It does not use generative AI for any reading. The text you see is rule output and hand-written guides.
