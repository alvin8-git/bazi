The Five Elements in a BaZi Chart: Balance, the 生克 Cycles, and What Excess Means

By BaziMaster 2026-09-19 Updated 2026-10-09

BaZi treats a chart as a five-element ecosystem of 木 wood, 火 fire, 土 earth, 金 metal and 水 water. Every stem, branch and hidden stem carries one element, and a weighted count of all of them gives the chart's element balance.

How are the five elements counted in a chart?

The engine gives each stem, including the Day Master, a weight of 1.0. Each branch adds 1.0 for its main hidden stem and one third for each minor hidden stem (method note). It then adds the weights per element. The sample chart is 王大明, a fictional man born 1988-03-15 at 09:30 in Singapore, with pillars 戊辰 乙卯 己巳 戊辰 and Day Master 己.

His weights total 10.0: Wood 2.67, Fire 1.0, Earth 5.33, Metal 0.33 and Water 0.67. As shares, Earth is 53.3 %, Wood 26.7 %, Fire 10.0 %, Water 6.7 % and Metal 3.3 %. The habit that matters most is to read these as balance, not as a score. The tallest bar is the heaviest influence, not the best one, and the shortest bar is where the ecosystem is thin.

Key facts

  • The five elements are 木, 火, 土, 金 and 水, and every stem and branch belongs to one of them.
  • The feeding cycle (生) runs 木→火→土→金→水→木, and the controlling cycle (剋) runs 木→土→水→火→金→木.
  • The sample's weights are Wood 2.67, Fire 1.0, Earth 5.33, Metal 0.33 and Water 0.67, totalling 10.0.
  • In shares, 土 is 53.3 % and 金 is only 3.3 %, which the engine bands as absent.
  • The engine finds two frictions: 土 over 水, and 木 over 土.
  • The favourable elements are 火 and 土 (扶抑法 and 調候法), with colours 紅, 紫, 黃 and 棕.

How do the two cycles work?

The elements interact along two fixed cycles. In the feeding cycle (相生), each element generates the next. Wood feeds fire, fire makes earth, earth bears metal, metal carries water, and water nourishes wood. In the controlling cycle (相剋), each element checks another, the way a dam checks a river.

Both cycles are neutral machinery. Control is structure, not harm. Trouble appears when weights are lopsided. A very heavy element crushes the one it controls, which is a friction. A very weak element cannot feed the one downstream of it, which is a gap. The engine attaches a fixed sentence to each pair.

What are the five roles around your Day Master?

Relative to your Day Master, the other four elements take fixed roles. The element that feeds you is your resource (印). The one you produce is your output (食傷). The one you control is your wealth (財). The one that controls you is your pressure or authority (官殺). Your own element is your peers (比劫).

For the sample, the Day Master 己 is Earth, so the roles fall as follows.

What does the sample chart's balance mean?

The engine writes its findings as short sentences, each tied to a cycle. For the sample it reports five.

  • 土 dominates at 53.3 %, so steady, reliable, grounding colours the whole chart, and its excesses are the first faults to watch.
  • 金 is effectively absent at 3.3 %, so structure and follow-through need deliberate effort. The chain into 水 also loses its feeder.
  • 土 at 53.3 % bears down on weak 水 at 6.7 %, so caution dams flow and communication gets blocked.
  • 木 at 26.7 % stands against 土 at 53.3 %, so growth overruns stability. The engine calls this a recurring theme, manageable once named.
  • The support line is intact: 火 at 10.0 % feeds 土, so help and recovery are structurally available.

Absence has a fixed cost in the engine's wording. A missing 金 means structure and follow-through need deliberate effort. A missing 木 would mean initiative and fresh starts need deliberate effort. A missing 水 would mean adaptability and communication need deliberate effort. None of these is a curse. Each marks a job that does not run on autopilot.

How do elements map to health and daily choices?

Traditional five-element medicine links each element to organ systems. The engine marks a very weak or very heavy element as a system worth caring for. In the sample, 土 is flagged as excess (過旺), covering spleen, stomach, digestion and overthinking. 金 and 水 are flagged as weak (不足), covering lungs, skin, kidneys and bones. Wood and Fire are balanced. This is a reference layer and never medical advice.

The practical levers are gentler. The engine judges the Day Master weak (身弱, score -0.97), so its medicine feeds and protects: 火 and 土. These translate into colours (紅, 紫, 黃, 棕), room sectors and work settings. The favourable elements guide explains that step.

What changes for Singapore and Malaysia readers?

The element count depends on the four pillars, so anything that shifts a pillar shifts the balance.

  • Year boundary. The BaZi year turns at 立春, which falls on 2027-02-04 in 2027. A birth in late January or early February may belong to the previous year, with a different year pillar and a different count.
  • Time zone. Singapore used UTC+7:30 before 1982-01-01 and UTC+8 after it. Malaysia has used UTC+8 since 1982-01-01. A wrong offset can move the hour pillar and change two of the eight characters.
  • True solar time. The engine converts the clock to true solar time. The sample's 09:30 became 08:16 before the hour pillar was cast.
  • Homes. For an HDB flat or condominium unit, element advice applies to what you control, such as the colour of a study wall or bedding. You do not need to renovate.
