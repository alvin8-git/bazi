Bed Placement and Direction: The Classical Rules That Actually Bind

2026-09-16 · bazifor.me Learn · Fengshui

No fengshui topic generates more contradictory advice than bed placement. The confusion dissolves once you separate the two rule systems at work: compass rules (which direction should the head point, from your Eight House map) and form rules (what the door, windows, and walls physically do to the bed). Classical doctrine is explicit about precedence: 形势为先 — form first. A perfect compass direction does not redeem a bed the door slams into.

The form rules, in order of severity

  1. Solid wall behind the headboard. The head needs backing (靠). A headboard against glass — a window, and yes, a bay window — fails this rule; if a window wall is unavoidable, a tall solid headboard and heavy curtains are the mitigation, and a platform build should keep the pillow on solid wall with the glass along the bed's flank.
  2. Stay out of the door's entry line (门冲床). The strip of space the doorway projects into the room should not run onto the bed — worst of all onto the pillow. Remedy when the room forces it: a wardrobe, tall shelf, or desk stationed between door and bed to intercept the line.
  3. Feet not pointing squarely at the door. The classical 棺材位 aversion; a foot bench or low cabinet at the bed's foot settles mild cases.
  4. Commanding position (命令位). From the pillow you should see the door, ideally diagonally. A door hidden behind your head is the pattern to avoid; a door in view is safety the body actually registers.
  5. 床在门后 — bed crowded behind the door leaf. When the opened door stands beside the pillow, use a soft-close hinge or doorstop plus a bedside table as buffer.
  6. No mirror facing the bed, nothing heavy overhead (beams, shelves over the pillow), and no toilet sharing the headboard wall.

Then the compass rule

With the form constraints satisfied, point the headboard toward one of your four auspicious Eight House directions — 生气 (vitality) is the classic first choice, 天医 (health) and 延年 (relationships) close behind. Note the convention: the direction is where the top of your head points while lying down, not where your feet point. Couples with opposite east/west groups pick the wall that serves the partner under more strain, and compensate the other with their side of the bed and bedside arrangements.

A worked method you can apply tonight

  1. List the walls that give solid backing.
  2. Strike out walls in the door's entry line or swing.
  3. Of what remains, choose the wall whose direction scores best on your Eight House map.
  4. If no wall survives steps 1–3 cleanly, take the best compass wall and apply the named remedy for its specific defect — every defect above has one.
