Luck Cycles (大運) and Annual Windows: The Climate and Weather of a BaZi Chart

By BaziMaster 2026-09-19 Updated 2026-10-08

A birth chart is fixed; life is not. BaZi models time with two moving layers. The luck cycles 大運 are ten-year pillars, derived from the month pillar and stepped forward or backward by sex and year polarity — each decade overlays one stem and one branch on the natal chart. The annual pillar 流年 changes every 立春 (around February 4th) and overlays one more. The decade is the climate; the year is the weather inside that climate.

Grading a decade

Each decade's two characters are read against the chart's favourable and unfavourable elements, plus the interactions they form with the natal pillars (combinations, clashes, punishments). The result is a phase label: a growth decade (both characters helpful), a corrective one (both against the grain — a decade for maintenance, learning and pruning rather than big bets), or consolidation and transition phases in between. Two habits keep this honest. First, a corrective decade is not a bad life — it is a season with different work. Second, the decade brings its own ten gods: a chart missing an authority star can borrow one from a 官殺 decade for ten years, and behave accordingly.

Annual windows — four dimensions

Within a decade, each year is checked per life dimension rather than given one verdict: career (does the year activate or clash the month pillar; do authority stars arrive), wealth (do wealth stars arrive — and can the chart hold them; is it a 財庫 vault-opening year), relationship (桃花 peach-blossom years and activations of the spouse palace, the day branch), and health (years that feed an excess or replenish a weak element). A window is a tailwind, a caution is a navigation note; neither is a prediction.

The vault, the peach blossom, and other timed stars

Some chart features exist only as timing: the wealth vault 財庫 is the storage branch of your wealth element (one of 辰戌丑未) — present-but-sealed vaults open in their clash years, which are classic accumulation windows. The peach blossom 桃花 branch governs attraction years; the scholar star 文昌 favours exam and publication years. This is why two people with similar charts diverge: their calendars strike different pillars at different ages.

Read the decade band first (where am I in the arc), then the current year's four flags, then plan against the next green column — that is the whole method, and every flag in our readings cites the rule that raised it.
