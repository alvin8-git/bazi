Luck Cycles (大運) and Annual Windows: The Climate and Weather of a BaZi Chart

By BaziMaster 2026-09-19 Updated 2026-10-09

A birth chart is fixed, and BaZi models time with two moving layers on top of it. The luck cycles (大運) are ten-year pillars derived from the month pillar, and the annual pillar (流年) changes every 立春. The decade is the climate, and the year is the weather inside it.

How are luck cycles worked out?

A luck cycle is a stem and branch pair that overlays the natal chart for ten years. The sequence starts from the month pillar and steps through the sixty-pair cycle. Two rules fix the direction and the start age.

The direction is 陽男陰女順行, 陰男陽女逆行. A yang-year man or a yin-year woman runs forward. A yin-year man or a yang-year woman runs backward. The start age is 起運 = 距節日數 / 3, which means the days from birth to the nearest 節 (a solar-term boundary), divided by three.

The sample is 王大明, a fictional man born 1988-03-15 at 09:30 in Singapore. His pillars are 戊辰 乙卯 己巳 戊辰, and his Day Master is 己. His year stem 戊 is yang, so he runs forward from the month pillar 乙卯. The count goes to the 節 of 清明, and the start age comes out at 6.7.

  • Luck cycles step from the month pillar, forward or backward by the rule 陽男陰女順行, 陰男陽女逆行.
  • The start age is 起運 = 距節日數 / 3, and the sample's is 6.7 (counted to the 節 清明).

  • The 2027 year pillar 丁未 starts at 立春 on 2027-02-04, with career and health cautions and a study window.
  • The year 2030 (庚戌) clashes open the sample's sealed wealth vault 辰.

How are annual windows read?

Within a decade, each year is checked per life area, not given one verdict. The engine looks at career, wealth, relationship, health, study and movement. Each area shows one of three flags: a window, a caution, or quiet.

A window is a tailwind and a caution is a navigation note. Neither is a prediction. The flags come from fixed rules, such as the year branch combining with or clashing a natal pillar. Table 2 shows the sample's years from 2027 to 2030.

What are the vault, the peach blossom and other timed stars?

Some chart features exist only as timing. The wealth vault (財庫) is the storage branch of your wealth element. The sample's wealth element is 水, and its vault is 辰, which is present in his chart but sealed. A vault opens in the year whose branch clashes it. For 辰 that is any 戌 year, so 庚戌 in 2030 is flagged as a classical accumulation year.

The peach blossom (桃花) branch governs attraction years. For the sample, whose year branch 辰 sits in the 申·子·辰 group, the peach blossom branch is 酉. The 2029 year 己酉 therefore carries the relationship window. The symbolic stars guide lists the lookup tables behind these stars.

This is why two people with similar charts diverge. Their calendars strike different pillars at different ages.

How do you use the timeline in practice?

Read the decade band first, to see where you are in the arc. Then read the current year's flags, area by area. Then plan against the next window. A short sequence works for most people.

  1. Find the current decade and its phase. For the sample in 2026, at age 38, it is 己未, growth.
  2. Check the year pillar for the year you are planning. For 2027 it is 丁未.
  3. Look only at the area you care about, such as career or study.
  4. Treat a caution as a reason to pace the decision, not to cancel it.

What changes for Singapore and Malaysia readers?

The luck sequence depends on exact birth data, so local time rules matter.

  • Year boundary. The annual pillar changes at 立春, not on 1 January. 立春 falls on 2026-02-04, 2027-02-04 and 2028-02-04. A birth in late January or early February may belong to the earlier year. That changes the year stem, its polarity and the direction of the luck cycles.
  • Time zone. Singapore ran on UTC+7:30 before 1982-01-01 and UTC+8 after it. Malaysia has used UTC+8 since 1982-01-01. A wrong offset can move the birth across a 節 boundary, which changes the start age.
  • Start age. The start age depends on the distance to the 節, so a birth close to a boundary can shift it by months.
  • Moves. The movement flag is one input if you are changing homes. For an HDB flat or condominium unit, the move-in day itself is chosen by a separate filter, described in move-in date selection.
