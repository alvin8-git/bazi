Move-In Date (入宅) Selection: Day Officers, Clashes and 2027 Dates

By BaziMaster 2026-09-16 Updated 2026-10-08

Move-in date selection (入宅擇日) is a filter over the calendar. It keeps days whose 建除 officer is 成, 定 or 開, then removes days whose branch clashes with the occupants or the house. Because the filters are fixed rules, a computer can run them exactly and a careful almanac reader gets the same days.

How does the day-officer rule work?

Every day carries one of twelve officers (建除十二神). The officer is the offset of the day's branch from the month's branch: the day whose branch equals the month branch is 建, the next branch is 除, and so on round the cycle of twelve. This is the 十二建除 rule of the 協紀辨方書 tradition.

For moving in, the engine keeps three officers: 成 (completion), 定 (settling) and 開 (opening). Everything else is passed over. A day whose branch clashes the month branch is 破 (月破), the hard avoid.

Key facts

  • The 12 day officers run from 建 to 閉, and the engine keeps 3 of them for 入宅: 成, 定 and 開.
  • Officer = (day branch minus month branch) modulo 12, counted from 建.
  • A move-in day is also dropped if its branch clashes an occupant's year branch, clashes the sitting branch or equals the year's 太歲 branch.
  • In 2027 the 太歲 branch is 未 (SW1, 202.5-217.5 degrees) and the year starts at 立春, 2027-02-04.
  • For the sample household, facing 14.0 degrees, the engine returns 9 auspicious days between 2027-03-01 and 2027-04-30.
  • The first of those is 2027-03-04, a 壬午 day with officer 定.

What are the twelve officers worth?

The engine's 擇日 layer rates each officer with a score (method note: these are the engine's weights, not a classical table). The move-in filter itself is simpler: it keeps 成, 定 and 開 and orders them 成 first, then 定, then 開.

Which days are struck out by clashes?

Each branch clashes with the one opposite it: 子 and 午, 丑 and 未, 寅 and 申, 卯 and 酉, 辰 and 戌, 巳 and 亥. After the officer filter the engine drops a day when its branch:

  1. Clashes any occupant's year branch (六沖), so the move does not collide with anyone who will live there.
  2. Clashes the sitting branch (沖坐山), the branch of the direction the house sits toward, derived from the facing bearing.
  3. Equals the year's 太歲 branch (值太歲). In 2027 that branch is 未.

The engine can also rate a day for each household member, using a clash with their year branch (沖生肖), a clash with their day-pillar branch, and a 六合 bonus. Those are softer, personal layers on top of the move-in filter.

Last, the engine can return entry hours for a chosen day, computed from the day branch, the occupants' branches and the facing.

You can check the officers by hand with the offset rule. On 2027-03-11 the month branch is 卯 and the day branch is 丑, which sits 10 places on from 卯 in the cycle of twelve, and position 10 is 開. On 2027-04-11 the month branch is 辰 and the day branch is 申, 4 places on, which is 定. Other days in the engine's output show why most are passed over: 2027-03-06 (甲申) is 執, 2027-03-15 (癸巳) is 滿 and 2027-04-08 (丁巳) is 除.

Nine days out of a 61-day window is a short list, which is why the window should be generous. The filter knows nothing about your leave days, lift bookings or key-collection date, so choose the window first, take the engine's list, then fit practical constraints to it. If none of the nine works, widen the window rather than relaxing a clash rule.

What changes for Singapore and Malaysia homes?

  • Facing. For an HDB flat or condominium unit, take the facing from the unit's main door, outward, as a bearing in degrees. The sitting branch follows from it, so a different bearing can change which days survive.
  • Year boundary. The 2027 year begins at 立春 on 2027-02-04. The positions below apply to the 丁未 year that opens on that date.
  • 2027 year positions. 太歲 is at 未, SW1. 歲破 is at 丑, NE1, 22.5-37.5 degrees. 三煞 covers the west, 232.5-307.5 degrees. A door facing 14.0 degrees lies outside all three bands. These are rule-derived positions from the 年神 rules of the 協紀辨方書.
  • Time. Dates here follow the calendar as set by the solar terms, which the engine takes from an astronomical library. Singapore and Malaysia both use UTC+8 today.

Sources and method

  • Day officers: the 十二建除 rule, with the officer set by the day branch's offset from the month branch.
  • Kept officers: 成, 定 and 開 for 入宅, the engine's convention (method note).
  • Month breaker: 月破, the day whose branch clashes the month branch, treated as a hard avoid.
  • Clash filters: the 六沖 pairs applied to occupants' year branches, the sitting branch and the 太歲 branch.
  • 2027 positions: the 年神 position rules of 協紀辨方書, applied to the 丁未 year.
  • Scope: the engine does not run the full almanac layers (twenty-eight mansions, individual stars), so its verdict may differ from a printed almanac (method note).
