Choosing a Move-In Date (入宅择日): The Classical Method, Step by Step

2026-09-16 · bazifor.me Learn · Dates

Date selection (择日) is the most immediately practical branch of the classical toolkit, and moving into a home (入宅) is its most requested application. It is also fully computable — a filter pipeline over the calendar, applied in a fixed order. Here is the method, exactly as an engine (or a careful almanac reader) runs it.

Step 1: the twelve day officers (建除十二神)

Every day carries one of twelve officers, determined by the relationship between the day's branch and the month's branch. For moving in, three officers are the classic keepers:

  • 成 (Completion) — the strongest choice: things established endure.
  • 定 (Settling) — stability; excellent for taking residence.
  • 开 (Opening) — beginnings; good, slightly behind the other two.

Days carrying 破 (Destruction), 闭 (Closing) and most others are simply passed over for this purpose. This first filter alone removes about three-quarters of the calendar.

Step 2: strike the clash days (六冲)

Each Earthly Branch clashes with the branch opposite it (子↔午, 丑↔未, 寅↔申, 卯↔酉, 辰↔戌, 巳↔亥). Three clash checks apply:

  1. Every occupant's year branch. A day whose branch clashes any family member's birth-year branch is out — the move should not collide with anyone who will live there.
  2. The house's sitting (冲坐山). A day clashing the branch of the direction your home sits toward disturbs the foundation of the very thing you are establishing. (A sitting on a stem or trigram mountain borrows its palace's branch.)
  3. The year's 太岁 (值太岁). A day whose branch matches the year branch confronts the Grand Duke — also out.

Step 3: the customs that complete it

With candidate dates in hand, tradition adds the finishing conventions: move in during the morning — the 巳时 block (9–11 am) is the customary favourite — carry something of value in first, and light the stove the same day: boil water, cook something simple. In the classical view the burning stove, not the furniture, is what declares occupancy. And if renovation preceded the move, the annual affliction sectors (太岁 / 岁破 / 三煞 / 五黄) should already have dictated when each room's work happened — date selection and renovation timing are two halves of one calendar discipline.

Why weekends thin out

Run the full pipeline over a three-month window and you will typically keep six to twelve days — and only a couple land on weekends, which is why families who need a Saturday should scan a generous window early. The filters are unforgiving in a specific, checkable way: a large family protects more branches, so their calendar is thinner. That is not superstition compounding; it is set intersection.
