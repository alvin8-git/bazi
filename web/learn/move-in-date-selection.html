Move-In Date (move-in, 入宅 rù zhái) Selection: Day Officers, Clashes and 2027 Dates

By BaziMaster 2026-09-16 Updated 2026-10-08

Move-in date selection (入宅擇日 rù zhái zé rì) is a filter over the calendar. It keeps days whose day officer (建除 jiàn chú) is Completion (成 chéng), Settle (定 dìng) or Open (開 kāi), then removes days whose branch clashes with the occupants or the house. Because the filters are fixed rules, a computer can run them exactly and a careful almanac reader gets the same days.

How does the day-officer rule work?

Every day carries one of twelve officers (twelve day officers, 建除十二神 jiàn chú shí èr shén). The officer is the offset of the day's branch from the month's branch: the day whose branch equals the month branch is Establish (建 jiàn), the next branch is Remove (除 chú), and so on round the cycle of twelve. This is the twelve day officers (十二建除 shí èr jiàn chú) rule of the 協紀辨方書 (xié jì biàn fāng shū, Qing almanac manual) tradition.

For moving in, the engine keeps three officers: Completion, Settle and Open. Everything else is passed over. A day whose branch clashes the month branch is a Month Breaker (月破 yuè pò) day, the hard avoid.

Key facts

  • The 12 day officers run from Establish to Close (閉 bì), and the engine keeps 3 of them for move-in: Completion, Settle and Open.
  • Officer = (day branch minus month branch) modulo 12, counted from Establish.
  • A move-in day is also dropped if its branch clashes an occupant's year branch, clashes the sitting branch or equals the year's Grand Duke (太歲 tài suì) branch.
  • In 2027 the Grand Duke branch is 未 (wèi, SW1, 202.5-217.5 degrees) and the year starts at Start of Spring (立春 lì chūn), 2027-02-04.
  • For the sample household, facing 14.0 degrees, the engine returns 9 auspicious days between 2027-03-01 and 2027-04-30.
  • The first of those is 2027-03-04, a 壬午 (rén wǔ, Water Horse) day with officer Settle.

The engine's date selection (擇日 zé rì) layer rates each officer with a score (method note: these are the engine's weights, not a classical table). The move-in filter itself is simpler: it keeps Completion, Settle and Open and orders them Completion first, then Settle, then Open.

Which days are struck out by clashes?

Each branch clashes with the one opposite it: 子 (zǐ, Rat, Water) and 午 (wǔ, Horse, Fire), 丑 (chǒu, Ox, Earth) and 未 wèi, 寅 (yín, Tiger, Wood) and 申 (shēn, Monkey, Metal), 卯 (mǎo, Rabbit, Wood) and 酉 (yǒu, Rooster, Metal), 辰 (chén, Dragon, Earth) and 戌 (xū, Dog, Earth), 巳 (sì, Snake, Fire) and 亥 (hài, Pig, Water). After the officer filter the engine drops a day when its branch:

  1. Clashes any occupant's year branch (Six Clash, 六沖 liù chōng), so the move does not collide with anyone who will live there.
  2. Clashes the sitting branch (clashes the sitting, 沖坐山 chōng zuò shān), the branch of the direction the house sits toward, derived from the facing bearing.
  3. Equals the year's Grand Duke branch (meets the Grand Duke, 值太歲 zhí tài suì). In 2027 that branch is 未 wèi.

The engine can also rate a day for each household member, using a clash with their year branch (clashes the zodiac animal, 沖生肖 chōng shēng xiào), a clash with their day-pillar branch, and a Six Harmony (六合 liù hé) bonus. Those are softer, personal layers on top of the move-in filter.

Last, the engine can return entry hours for a chosen day, computed from the day branch, the occupants' branches and the facing.

Nine days out of a 61-day window is a short list, which is why the window should be generous. The filter knows nothing about your leave days, lift bookings or key-collection date, so choose the window first, take the engine's list, then fit practical constraints to it. If none of the nine works, widen the window rather than relaxing a clash rule.

What changes for Singapore and Malaysia homes?

  • Facing. For an HDB flat or condominium unit, take the facing from the unit's main door, outward, as a bearing in degrees. The sitting branch follows from it, so a different bearing can change which days survive.
  • Year boundary. The 2027 year begins at Start of Spring on 2027-02-04. The positions below apply to the 丁未 (dīng wèi, Fire Goat) year that opens on that date.
  • 2027 year positions. Grand Duke is at 未 wèi, SW1. Year Breaker (歲破 suì pò) is at 丑 chǒu, NE1, 22.5-37.5 degrees. Three Killings (三煞 sān shà) covers the west, 232.5-307.5 degrees. A door facing 14.0 degrees lies outside all three bands. These are rule-derived positions from the year spirits (年神 nián shén) rules of the 協紀辨方書 xié jì biàn fāng shū.
  • Time. Dates here follow the calendar as set by the solar terms, which the engine takes from an astronomical library. Singapore and Malaysia both use UTC+8 today.

FAQ

How is a move-in date chosen in feng shui?

Move-in date selection is a filter over the calendar. It keeps days whose day officers officer is Completion, Settle or Open. It then removes days whose branch clashes with the occupants or the house. Fixed rules mean a careful almanac reader gets the same days.

What are the twelve day officers?

Every day carries one of twelve officers (twelve day officers), set by the day branch's offset from the month branch. The day whose branch equals the month branch is Establish, and the next is Remove. For moving in, only Completion, Settle and Open are kept.

Which days are struck out for a house move?

After the officer filter, a day is dropped if its branch clashes an occupant's year branch or the sitting branch. A day is also dropped if it equals the year's Grand Duke branch. In 2027 the Grand Duke branch is 未 wèi. A day that clashes the month branch is destruction, the hard avoid.

What do I need to give for a move-in date in Singapore or Malaysia?

You need the occupants' year branches, the facing bearing in degrees and a date window. For an HDB flat or condo, take the facing from the unit's main door, outward. A different bearing can change which days survive.

What if none of the suggested days works for my schedule?

The filter knows nothing about your leave days, lift bookings or key collection date. Choose the window first, take the list, then fit practical constraints to it. If none works, widen the window rather than relaxing a clash rule.

Sources and method

  • Day officers: the twelve day officers rule, with the officer set by the day branch's offset from the month branch.
  • Kept officers: Completion, Settle and Open for move-in, the engine's convention (method note).
  • Month breaker: Month Breaker, the day whose branch clashes the month branch, treated as a hard avoid.
  • Clash filters: the Six Clash pairs applied to occupants' year branches, the sitting branch and the Grand Duke branch.
  • 2027 positions: the year spirits position rules of 協紀辨方書 xié jì biàn fāng shū, applied to the 丁未 dīng wèi year.
  • Scope: the engine does not run the full almanac layers (twenty-eight mansions, individual stars), so its verdict may differ from a printed almanac (method note).
