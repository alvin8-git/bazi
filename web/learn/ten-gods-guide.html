Ten Gods (十神) Explained: Meanings, Groups and Luck Cycles for 己

By BaziMaster 2026-09-19 Updated 2026-10-08

The Ten Gods (十神) are the ten relationships any stem can have to your Day Master. Each is a pair of one element role split by yin and yang polarity: peers, output, wealth, authority and resource. Every visible or hidden stem, and every luck pillar, falls into one of the ten.

What are the ten gods, and how are they grouped?

They come in five pairs, one pair per elemental role. The engine names the groups 比劫 (peers and independence), 食傷 (output and expression), 財 (wealth), 官殺 (authority) and 印 (resource). The 正 god of a pair is the conventional, steady flavour; its partner is the intense, unconventional one, powerful when channelled and costly when not.

Which god a stem is depends on your Day Master. Table 1 is the full list for Day Master 己, with the group and the engine's meaning for each god.

Key facts

  • There are 10 gods in 5 groups: 比劫, 食傷, 財, 官殺 and 印.
  • For Day Master 己, the stem 甲 is 正官 and the stem 乙 is 七殺.
  • The sample chart's distribution is 劫財 48.1 %, 七殺 29.6 %, 正印 11.1 %, 偏財 7.4 % and 傷官 3.7 %.
  • The sample's current luck pillar is 己未 for ages 37-47, with 比肩 on both stem and branch.
  • The luck sequence runs forward from the month pillar 乙卯 and starts at age 6.7, by the rule 陽男陰女順行 with 起運 = distance to the 節 divided by 3.
  • The 2027 year pillar is 丁未 from 立春, 2027-02-04, and 丁 is 偏印 for a 己 Day Master.

Wealth and authority carry a second meaning in the classics: 財 is the spouse star in a man's chart and 官殺 is the spouse star in a woman's chart. The gods also stand for people (六親): wealth for the father, resource for the mother and mentors, peers for siblings.

What are the bright and shadow sides of each god?

Every god has a bright side and a shadow side. The shadow shows when the god is excessive or unsupported, so a prominent god is both your engine and your watch-list.

The classical "four benign" gods are 正官, 正印, 正財 and 食神. The "four fierce" are 七殺, 傷官, 偏印 and 劫財.

How do you read a distribution chart?

A distribution chart weighs every visible and hidden stem and shows how the chart's energy divides among the ten. Three habits make it useful.

  1. Heavy bars are engine and watch-list. A god with a large share is real strength and real pressure at once.
  2. Empty bars are not doom. A missing god is a domain that does not run on autopilot, not a curse. No 比劫 means self-drive must be deliberate, and no 官殺 means structure has to be chosen.
  3. Percentages start the reading, structure finishes it. Whether the heavy god sits on a favourable element, whether it is rooted in a branch, and what the gods form together decide how the weight behaves.

How do the gods arrive with time?

The chart is the cast and the calendar is the schedule. Each ten-year luck pillar (大運) carries a stem god and a branch god for your Day Master. The sample runs forward because the rule is 陽男陰女順行 (yang man, yin woman forward), and it starts at age 6.7, counted as the distance to the next 節 divided by 3.

What changes for Singapore and Malaysia readers?

Annual years bring their own god. The 2027 year pillar is 丁未, and 丁 is 偏印 for a 己 Day Master, so the year carries unconventional learning and niche skills. The year turns at 立春, 2027-02-04, so a Singapore or Malaysia birth in late January or early February may sit in the earlier year and change every god. Clock time matters too: Singapore ran on UTC+7:30 before 1982-01-01 and UTC+8 after it, and Malaysia has used UTC+8 since 1982-01-01. A shifted hour pillar changes the distribution.
