The Ten Gods (十神) Explained: What You Have, What You Lack, and Why It Matters

The reference our readings link to from the 十神 Gods tab.

Once the Day Master is fixed, every other character in a BaZi chart — visible or hidden — falls into one of ten relationships with it: the 十神 ten gods. They come in five pairs, one pair for each of the five elemental roles, split by yin-yang polarity:

  • 比肩 Friend · 劫財 Rob Wealth (peers 比劫) — self-identity, willpower, siblings, competition.
  • 食神 Eating God · 傷官 Hurting Officer (output 食傷) — expression, creativity, critique; your life's output.
  • 正財 Direct Wealth · 偏財 Indirect Wealth (wealth 財) — salary and savings; opportunity and business flair.
  • 正官 Direct Officer · 七殺 Seven Killings (authority 官殺) — rules, status, career, discipline, pressure.
  • 正印 Direct Resource · 偏印 Indirect Resource (resource 印) — learning, protection, credentials, intuition.

The 正 gods are the conventional, steady flavour of each domain; their counterparts are the intense, unconventional flavour — powerful when channelled, costly when not. The classical "four benign" are 正官·正印·正財·食神; the "four fierce" are 七殺·傷官·偏印·劫財.

Reading a distribution chart

A ten-god distribution chart weighs every visible and hidden stem and shows how the chart's energy divides among the ten. Three habits make it useful. First, read the heavy bars as both engine and watch-list: a god's shadow side activates when it is excessive or unsupported — 26% of 正官 is real authority and real self-imposed pressure. Second, read the empty bars: a missing god is a life-domain that does not run on autopilot. No 比劫 means self-drive must be deliberate; no 官殺 means structure has to be chosen, not imposed. Missing is not doomed — it is unautomated. Third, percentages start the reading, structure finishes it: whether the heavy god sits on a favourable element for you, whether it is rooted in a branch, and what the gods form together (patterns like 傷官見官, talent chafing against authority) decide how the weight actually behaves.

The cycle as a tree

A memorable way to hold the five groups at once: 比劫 are the roots absorbing nutrients, 食傷 the branches and leaves that grow from them, 財 the flowers that set fruit, 官殺 the pickers who arrive for the harvest, and 印 the soil that rests and recovers — feeding new roots. Creation → accumulation → constraint → nourishment, around forever; every chart simply weights the stations of this cycle differently.

The gods as people

Each god also stands for people (六親): wealth gods for the father — and in a man's chart the wife; authority gods in a woman's chart for the husband; resource for the mother and mentors; output for students and, in a woman's chart, children; peers for siblings and rivals. That is why the same distribution chart doubles as a relationship map, and why the spouse star's strength and the spouse palace (the day branch) are read together.

Gods arrive with time

The chart is the cast; the calendar is the schedule. Every stem maps to a god for your Day Master, so each ten-year luck pillar and each passing year brings specific gods on stage. A decade that carries your missing god supplies it for ten years; a year that doubles an already-heavy god tests its shadow side. This is how a static birth chart produces a moving story — see luck cycles and windows.
