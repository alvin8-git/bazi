Ten Gods (十神) Explained: Meanings, Groups and Luck Cycles for 己 (yin Earth)

By BaziMaster 2026-09-19 Updated 2026-10-08

The Ten Gods are the ten relationships any stem can have to your Day Master. Each is a pair of one element role split by yin and yang polarity: peers, output, wealth, authority and resource. Every visible or hidden stem, and every luck pillar, falls into one of the ten.

What are the ten gods, and how are they grouped?

They come in five pairs, one pair per elemental role. The engine names the groups peers (比劫), output (食傷), wealth (財), authority (官殺) and resource (印). The 正 god of a pair is the conventional, steady flavour; its partner is the intense, unconventional one, powerful when channelled and costly when not.

Which god a stem is depends on your Day Master. Table 1 is the full list for Day Master 己, with the group and the engine's meaning for each god.

Key facts

  • There are 10 gods in 5 groups: peers, output, wealth, authority and resource.
  • For Day Master 己, the stem 甲 (yang Wood) is Direct Officer (正官) and the stem 乙 (yin Wood) is Seven Killings (七殺).
  • The sample chart's distribution is Rob Wealth (劫財) 48.1 %, Seven Killings 29.6 %, Direct Resource (正印) 11.1 %, Indirect Wealth (偏財) 7.4 % and Hurting Officer (傷官) 3.7 %.
  • The sample's current luck pillar is 己未 (Earth Goat) for ages 37-47, with Friend (比肩) on both stem and branch.
  • The luck sequence runs forward from the month pillar 乙卯 (Wood Rabbit) and starts at age 6.7, by the rule yang men and yin women count forward (陽男陰女順行) with luck-cycle start (起運) = distance to the solar-term boundary (節) divided by 3.
  • The 2027 year pillar is 丁未 (Fire Goat) from Start of Spring (立春), 2027-02-04, and 丁 (yin Fire) is Indirect Resource (偏印) for a 己 Day Master.

What are the bright and shadow sides of each god?

Every god has a bright side and a shadow side. The shadow shows when the god is excessive or unsupported, so a prominent god is both your engine and your watch-list.

The classical "four benign" gods are Direct Officer, Direct Resource, Direct Wealth and Eating God. The "four fierce" are Seven Killings, Hurting Officer, Indirect Resource and Rob Wealth.

How do you read a distribution chart?

A distribution chart weighs every visible and hidden stem and shows how the chart's energy divides among the ten. Three habits make it useful.

  1. Heavy bars are engine and watch-list. A god with a large share is real strength and real pressure at once.
  2. Empty bars are not doom. A missing god is a domain that does not run on autopilot, not a curse. No peers means self-drive must be deliberate, and no authority means structure has to be chosen.
  3. Percentages start the reading, structure finishes it. Whether the heavy god sits on a favourable element, whether it is rooted in a branch, and what the gods form together decide how the weight behaves.

How do the gods arrive with time?

The chart is the cast and the calendar is the schedule. Each ten-year luck pillar (luck cycles, 大運) carries a stem god and a branch god for your Day Master. The sample runs forward because the rule is yang man, yin woman forward, and it starts at age 6.7, counted as the distance to the next solar-term boundary divided by 3.

What changes for Singapore and Malaysia readers?

Annual years bring their own god. The 2027 year pillar is 丁未, and 丁 is Indirect Resource for a 己 Day Master, so the year carries unconventional learning and niche skills. The year turns at Start of Spring, 2027-02-04, so a Singapore or Malaysia birth in late January or early February may sit in the earlier year and change every god. Clock time matters too: Singapore ran on UTC+7:30 before 1982-01-01 and UTC+8 after it, and Malaysia has used UTC+8 since 1982-01-01. A shifted hour pillar changes the distribution.
