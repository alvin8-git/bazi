Eight House (八宅) Basics: Your Four Good and Four Bad Directions

2026-09-16 · bazifor.me Learn · Fengshui

Of all the classical fengshui schools, Eight House (八宅) is the most personal: it starts not from the building but from you. From your birth year and sex it derives a life gua (命卦) — one of eight trigrams — and from that, every compass direction becomes one of eight qualities, four auspicious and four inauspicious. It is the system behind "sleep with your head pointing this way" and "face this direction at your desk."

Your life gua and your group

The 命卦 is computed from the birth year (with the 立春 boundary, as always) by a digital-root method, with separate formulas for men and women. The eight guas split into two families:

  • East group (东四命): 坎 (water), 离 (fire), 震 (wood), 巽 (wood)
  • West group (西四命): 乾 (metal), 坤 (earth), 艮 (earth), 兑 (metal)

East-group people share the same four good directions (N, S, E, SE); West-group people share the other four (W, NW, SW, NE). Which of the four is which quality differs by gua — that mapping is the 大游年 table, and it is pure lookup, no judgment involved.

The eight qualities

The four auspicious:

  • 生气 (Sheng Qi) — vitality; the strongest. Best for the headboard, the desk facing, the front door.
  • 天医 (Tian Yi) — the heavenly doctor; health and recovery.
  • 延年 (Yan Nian) — longevity and relationships; steady support.
  • 伏位 (Fu Wei) — the home position; calm and stability.

The four inauspicious, in worsening order: 祸害 (mishaps) → 六煞 (six killings) → 五鬼 (five ghosts) → 绝命 (severed fate). The practical rule is not fear but allocation: put activity in your good directions (sleeping, studying, facing) and storage or utility in the bad ones.

Using it in a real home

  1. Headboard direction first. Sleeping is a third of your life; pointing the head toward 生气, 天医 or 延年 is the highest-leverage move the school offers.
  2. Desk facing second. You face a direction for hours; make it an auspicious one.
  3. Couples with opposite groups are normal. One east, one west is common — the classical resolution is to favour the person carrying the heavier load in that room, and to compensate the other elsewhere (their side of the bed, their study corner).
  4. The house has a gua too. A dwelling's sitting direction makes it an east or west house; matching person to house is a bonus, not a requirement — room allocation does the real work.

Where 八宅 sits among the schools

Eight House answers "which directions suit this person"; Flying Stars (玄空飞星) answers "what energy occupies this sector of this building in this period"; form-school doctrine answers "what does the physical layout do" (doors, beds, sightlines). Serious practice layers all three, with form first (形势为先) when they conflict.
