Eight House (八宅) Directions: Your Kua and All Eight Direction Scores

By BaziMaster 2026-09-16 Updated 2026-10-08

Eight House (八宅) is a feng shui method that starts from the person, not the building. Your birth year and sex give a life gua (命卦, "kua"), one of eight trigrams, and the 八宅大遊年 table turns that kua into a quality for each of the eight compass directions: four auspicious, four inauspicious, each with an engine score.

What is a kua, and which group are you in?

The kua comes from the birth year, using the 立春 boundary and separate formulas for men and women. The eight kua then fall into two families. East group (東四命) is 坎, 離, 震 and 巽. West group (西四命) is 乾, 坤, 艮 and 兌. People in the same group share the same four good directions, though not the same ranking of them. Find your own number with the free Kua number calculator.

Key facts

  • The 8 kua split into an East group (坎, 離, 震, 巽) and a West group (乾, 坤, 艮, 兌).
  • The engine scores the 8 qualities from 生氣 at +3.0 down to 絕命 at -3.0.
  • The sample person, 王大明 (born 1988-03-15), has kua 震, East group.
  • For kua 震 the best direction is 離 (south) at 生氣, and the worst is 兌 (west) at 絕命.
  • In 2027 the annual 五黃 star sits in 坎, the north, and 立春 2027-02-04 starts that year.
  • The engine cites the grid to 八宅大遊年, the 八宅明鏡 tradition.

What are the eight directions worth?

The engine scores the qualities as 生氣 +3.0, 天醫 +2.5, 延年 +2.0, 伏位 +1.0, 禍害 -1.0, 六煞 -1.5, 五鬼 -2.0 and 絕命 -3.0 (method note: these are the engine's weights, not a classical table). 生氣 is vitality, the strongest choice for a headboard, desk or main door. 天醫 is health, 延年 is steady support, and 伏位 is the calm home position.

What does a worked example show?

王大明 was born 1988-03-15 as a man, and the table gives him kua 震, East group (東四命). Row 震 in Table 1 reads: 離 (south) 生氣 +3.0, 坎 (north) 天醫 +2.5, 巽 (southeast) 延年 +2.0 and 震 (east) 伏位 +1.0. His bad directions are 坤 (southwest) 禍害 -1.0, 艮 (northeast) 六煞 -1.5, 乾 (northwest) 五鬼 -2.0 and 兌 (west) 絕命 -3.0.

In 2027 the annual flying star 5 (五黃) sits in 坎, the north. For 王大明 that is his 天醫 direction at +2.5, so a bedroom or desk in the north would sit under an annual affliction in a good direction. The two are separate layers (see Flying Stars 2027). The 2027 年神 positions are 太歲 at 未 (SW1, 202.5-217.5 degrees), 歲破 at 丑 (NE1, 22.5-37.5 degrees) and 三煞 in the west, 232.5-307.5 degrees.

How do you apply it in an HDB flat or condo in Singapore or Malaysia?

Eight House is applied to rooms and to the entrance, so the first step is a bearing, not a mood.

  1. Take the main door as the facing. In an HDB flat or condominium unit the facing is conventionally taken from the unit's main door, outward. Measure the bearing in degrees.
  2. Set the bed head and the desk. Point the head of the bed toward a good direction for the sleeper, and seat the desk so the sitter faces one. A couple whose kua lie in different groups share no good direction, because the two groups use four different directions each.
  3. Check the annual layer. The 2027 year starts at 立春, 2027-02-04, so the 五黃 north sector counts from that date.
  4. Enter the birth year correctly. A birth in January or early February can belong to the earlier year.

FAQ

What is a kua number in feng shui?

Your kua (命卦) is one of eight trigrams worked out from your birth year and sex, using the 立春 boundary. It places you in the East group (坎, 離, 震, 巽) or the West group (乾, 坤, 艮, 兌).

What are the eight Eight House directions and their scores?

The engine scores them 生氣 +3.0, 天醫 +2.5, 延年 +2.0, 伏位 +1.0, 禍害 -1.0, 六煞 -1.5, 五鬼 -2.0 and 絕命 -3.0. These are the engine's own weights, not a classical table.

Which kua group am I in if I was born in January or February?

A birth in January or early February before 立春 uses the previous year's row. The kua pattern repeats every 9 years, and men and women use separate formulas, so enter the birth year and sex carefully.

How do I use Eight House in an HDB flat or condo in Singapore or Malaysia?

Take the main door as the facing and measure its bearing in degrees. Point the bed head toward a good direction for the sleeper. Seat the desk so the sitter faces one. Then check the annual layer.

Can a couple with different kua groups share a good direction?

No. A couple whose kua lie in different groups share no good direction, because each group uses a different set of four directions. The East and West groups have four auspicious directions each, and none overlap.
