Flying Stars 2027 (丁未年): The Annual Map and Where Not to Renovate

2026-09-16 · bazifor.me Learn · Fengshui

Every solar year (立春 to 立春 — 2027's begins on 4 February 2027), the nine flying stars re-deal themselves across the eight compass sectors, and three calendar afflictions rotate with the year branch. 2027 is a 丁未 year — yin fire over the 未 (goat) branch. Here is the annual map, computed by the same engine that powers our home-fengshui readings, and what it means in practice.

The 2027 annual chart

The annual star entering the centre in 2027 is 9. The full palace distribution:

  • Centre 中 — 9
  • Northwest 乾 — 1
  • West 兑 — 2 (illness star 病符)
  • Northeast 艮 — 3 (quarrel star)
  • South 离 — 4 (academic 文昌 star)
  • North 坎 — 5 (五黄, the yellow calamity star)
  • Southwest 坤 — 6
  • East 震 — 7
  • Southeast 巽 — 8

The three calendar afflictions

  • 太岁 (Grand Duke) — southwest, on the 未 branch. Classical rule: 可坐不可向 — sitting with your back to it is fine; avoid facing it for long stretches, and never break ground in its sector this year.
  • 岁破 (Year Breaker) — northeast, opposite the 太岁. The strictest sector for ground-breaking and demolition.
  • 三煞 (Three Killings) — the western arc (乾/兑/坤 palaces). Rule: 可向不可坐 — facing it is acceptable; avoid prolonged sitting toward it and avoid renovation there.

What to actually do

  1. Do not hack, drill, or dig (动土) in the SW, NE, W arc, or N this year. That is the combined 太岁 + 岁破 + 三煞 + 五黄 exclusion map for 2027. Screw-together furniture assembly is not 动土; structural work is.
  2. Planning renovations across the boundary? Sequence them: a sector clean in 2026 but afflicted in 2027 should be done before 4 February 2027; the reverse waits until after. This one scheduling habit costs nothing and respects every school.
  3. The north (坎) hosts 五黄 — the year's heaviest annual star. Keep that sector quiet: no renovation, no loud constant activity; the traditional remedy is still metal (a six-coin or brass object), which drains the star's earth.
  4. The south (离) hosts the 4 star — the annual academic star. A student's desk or reading corner in the south sector gets a free tailwind this year.
  5. West (兑) carries the 2 illness star — worth the same metal-drain treatment if a bedroom sits there.

Annual stars are a layer, not the whole story

The annual chart sits on top of your building's natal chart (set by its facing and construction period) and your personal Eight House directions. A sector can be natal-strong and annually afflicted, or vice versa; the reading that matters is the overlay.
