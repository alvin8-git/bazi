Useful God (用神 yòng shén) in BaZi: Find Your Favourable Elements and Colours

By BaziMaster 2026-09-16 Updated 2026-10-08

A useful god is the element a BaZi chart needs most, chosen by rule from the balance of the Day Master and the season. Four classical methods find it: support and restrain (扶抑 fú yì), climate adjustment (調候 tiáo hòu), bridging element (通關 tōng guān) and illness and remedy (病藥 bìng yào). Once it is known, it turns into practical choices such as colours, directions and room use.

How is the useful god chosen?

After the Four Pillars are cast and the Day Master's strength is assessed, classical BaZi asks the most practical question in the system: which element does this chart need? The usual English, "useful god", is a little grand. "Operative element" is closer to the sense. Colours, directions, timing and even room choices in home feng shui flow from it.

The classical toolkit has four techniques, and the engine cites which one it used:

  1. Support and restrain is the workhorse. A weak Day Master is supported by the element that generates it (resource, 印 yìn, the resource) and the element that matches it (peers, 比劫 bǐ jié, peers). A strong one is drained or disciplined by output (食傷 shí shāng), wealth (財 cái) or authority (官殺 guān shā).
  2. Climate adjustment looks at the season. A chart born in deep winter may need fire regardless of strength, and a blazing summer chart may need water. Season can override arithmetic when the imbalance is extreme.
  3. Bridging element applies when two camps of elements deadlock. The element that mediates between them unlocks the chart.
  4. Illness and medicine applies when one element dominates and distorts the chart. The element that checks it is the cure.

These are written rules, and two practitioners applying them honestly reach the same answer. That is why a deterministic engine can compute the useful god and name the technique behind it. The strength framework underneath is how strong each element is in the birth month (旺相休囚死 wàng xiàng xiū qiú sǐ) together with whether the Day Master has roots in the branches (通根 tōng gēn).

  • The five-element cycles are fixed: wood feeds fire, fire feeds earth, earth feeds metal, metal feeds water, and water feeds wood.

What does a worked example look like?

Take the fictional 王大明, a man born on 1988-03-15 at 09:30 Singapore clock time. True solar time for Singapore (UTC+8, longitude 103.8517°E, equation of time -9.4 minutes) is 08:16, so the hour pillar is counted in the 辰 (chén, Dragon, Earth) block. The pillars are 戊辰 wù chén, 乙卯 yǐ mǎo, 己巳 jǐ sì, 戊辰 wù chén, and the Day Master is 己 jǐ, yin Earth.

Strength. The month branch is 卯 (mǎo, Rabbit, Wood), wood, which restrains earth, so the Day Master is in the dead (死 sǐ) phase. Support totals 6.33 against a drain of 3.67, and the roots (rooting) are 辰 chén, 巳 (sì, Snake, Fire) and 辰 chén. The net score is -0.97: weak Day Master (身弱 shēn ruò), weak.

Technique. Technique 1 of 4, support-and-restrain method (扶抑法 fú yì fǎ), applies directly. A weak Day Master favours the resource element (resource, Fire) and the peer element (peers, Earth). Technique 2 of 4, climate-adjustment method (調候法 tiáo hòu fǎ), is also cited in the result. The useful god come out as Fire and Earth. The unfavourable elements are Wood, Water and Metal.

Colours. The favourable families are red and purple for fire, and yellow and brown for earth.

Which colours should I actually use?

Your favourable elements' colours are worth favouring where you spend sustained time: wardrobe staples, bedding, a study corner, a phone case you see two hundred times a day. Your unfavourable elements' colours are not poison; they are simply not doing you any favours in bulk. The classical advice is dosage, not prohibition. An unfavourable colour as an accent is background noise; as the whole bedroom wall it is a standing weight.

In a Singapore or Malaysian home, three practical limits apply.

  • Rented and HDB walls. A rented unit often cannot be repainted. Move the personal palette to what you can change: bedsheets, curtains, a rug, a lampshade and desk accessories.
  • Large surfaces versus small ones. Paint a feature wall or a cabinet front in a favourable colour before changing a whole room, and keep unfavourable colours to accents.
  • Room context. In home feng shui, a room's flying-star overlay can outrank the personal palette on walls and large surfaces, with the personal colours moving to textiles. Rules interact, and a good reading shows the precedence.

The same element logic reaches the bed direction guide, which uses your kua rather than your useful god for direction, and the flying stars guide, which adds the annual overlay.

Why do people get the useful god wrong?

  • Guessing from the Day Master alone. A water Day Master does not automatically "need" water. A strong 壬 (rén, yang Water) usually needs the opposite, because strength decides the direction of the remedy.
  • Treating the zodiac animal as the answer. The year branch is one-eighth of the chart.
  • Using the wrong hour. The hour pillar comes from true solar time. In 王大明's case the clock reads 09:30 but the sun reads 08:16, which changes the hour from 巳 sì to 辰 chén and therefore the roots and the strength score.
  • Using the wrong year for early February births. The year pillar changes at Start of Spring (立春 lì chūn), which is 2027-02-04 in 2027, and not at Chinese New Year.
  • Pre-1982 births. Singapore ran on UTC+7:30 before 1982-01-01 and UTC+8 after; Peninsular Malaysia is UTC+8 from 1982-01-01. The conversion must be applied before any pillar is cast.
