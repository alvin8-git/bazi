Useful God (用神) in BaZi: Find Your Favourable Elements and Colours

By BaziMaster 2026-09-16 Updated 2026-10-08

A useful god (用神) is the element a BaZi chart needs most, chosen by rule from the balance of the Day Master and the season. Four classical methods find it: 扶抑, 調候, 通關 and 病藥. Once it is known, it turns into practical choices such as colours, directions and room use.

How is the 用神 chosen?

After the Four Pillars are cast and the Day Master's strength is assessed, classical BaZi asks the most practical question in the system: which element does this chart need? The usual English, "useful god", is a little grand. "Operative element" is closer to the sense. Colours, directions, timing and even room choices in home feng shui flow from it.

The classical toolkit has four techniques, and the engine cites which one it used:

  1. 扶抑 (support and restrain) is the workhorse. A weak Day Master is supported by the element that generates it (印, the resource) and the element that matches it (比劫, peers). A strong one is drained or disciplined by output (食傷), wealth (財) or authority (官殺).
  2. 調候 (climate adjustment) looks at the season. A chart born in deep winter may need fire regardless of strength, and a blazing summer chart may need water. Season can override arithmetic when the imbalance is extreme.
  3. 通關 (bridging) applies when two camps of elements deadlock. The element that mediates between them unlocks the chart.
  4. 病藥 (illness and medicine) applies when one element dominates and distorts the chart. The element that checks it is the cure.

These are written rules, and two practitioners applying them honestly reach the same answer. That is why a deterministic engine can compute the 用神 and name the technique behind it. The strength framework underneath is 旺相休囚死 (how strong each element is in the birth month) together with 通根 (whether the Day Master has roots in the branches).

Key facts

  • The 用神 is found by 4 techniques: 扶抑, 調候, 通關 and 病藥.
  • The five elements each have 2 colour families in the engine: wood 綠 青, fire 紅 紫, earth 黃 棕, metal 白 金, water 黑 藍.
  • In the engine's naming score, a name character that matches the 用神 earns 1.0, a 喜 element 0.7, a neutral one 0.3 and a 忌 element 0.0.
  • The sample chart 戊辰 乙卯 己巳 戊辰 has a weak Day Master 己 (score -0.97), and its 用神 are fire and earth.
  • The five-element cycles are fixed: wood feeds fire, fire feeds earth, earth feeds metal, metal feeds water, and water feeds wood.
  • The 三命通會 is the classical source for the 扶抑 reasoning the engine cites.

What does a worked example look like?

Take the fictional 王大明, a man born on 1988-03-15 at 09:30 Singapore clock time. True solar time for Singapore (UTC+8, longitude 103.8517°E, equation of time -9.4 minutes) is 08:16, so the hour pillar is counted in the 辰 block. The pillars are 戊辰, 乙卯, 己巳, 戊辰, and the Day Master is 己, yin Earth.

Strength. The month branch is 卯, wood, which restrains earth, so the Day Master is in the 死 phase. Support totals 6.33 against a drain of 3.67, and the roots (通根) are 辰, 巳 and 辰. The net score is -0.97: 身弱, weak.

Technique. Technique 1 of 4, 扶抑法, applies directly. A weak Day Master favours the resource element (印, Fire) and the peer element (比劫, Earth). Technique 2 of 4, 調候法, is also cited in the result. The 用神 come out as Fire and Earth. The unfavourable elements are Wood, Water and Metal.

Colours. The favourable families are 紅 and 紫 for fire, and 黃 and 棕 for earth.

Which colours should I actually use?

Your favourable elements' colours are worth favouring where you spend sustained time: wardrobe staples, bedding, a study corner, a phone case you see two hundred times a day. Your unfavourable elements' colours are not poison; they are simply not doing you any favours in bulk. The classical advice is dosage, not prohibition. An unfavourable colour as an accent is background noise; as the whole bedroom wall it is a standing weight.

In a Singapore or Malaysian home, three practical limits apply.

  • Rented and HDB walls. A rented unit often cannot be repainted. Move the personal palette to what you can change: bedsheets, curtains, a rug, a lampshade and desk accessories.
  • Large surfaces versus small ones. Paint a feature wall or a cabinet front in a favourable colour before changing a whole room, and keep unfavourable colours to accents.
  • Room context. In home feng shui, a room's flying-star overlay can outrank the personal palette on walls and large surfaces, with the personal colours moving to textiles. Rules interact, and a good reading shows the precedence.

The same element logic reaches the bed direction guide, which uses your kua rather than your 用神 for direction, and the flying stars guide, which adds the annual overlay.

Why do people get the 用神 wrong?

  • Guessing from the Day Master alone. A water Day Master does not automatically "need" water. A strong 壬 usually needs the opposite, because strength decides the direction of the remedy.
  • Treating the zodiac animal as the answer. The year branch is one-eighth of the chart.
  • Using the wrong hour. The hour pillar comes from true solar time. In 王大明's case the clock reads 09:30 but the sun reads 08:16, which changes the hour from 巳 to 辰 and therefore the roots and the strength score.
  • Using the wrong year for early February births. The year pillar changes at 立春, which is 2027-02-04 in 2027, and not at Chinese New Year.
  • Pre-1982 births. Singapore ran on UTC+7:30 before 1982-01-01 and UTC+8 after; Peninsular Malaysia is UTC+8 from 1982-01-01. The conversion must be applied before any pillar is cast.
