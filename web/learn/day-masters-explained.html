The Ten Day Masters (日主): Which One Are You?

2026-09-16 · bazifor.me Learn · BaZi Basics

In a BaZi chart, one character stands for you: the Heavenly Stem of your day pillar, called the Day Master (日主 or 日元). Everything else in the chart — the other seven characters, the luck cycles, the annual years — is read in relation to it. There are exactly ten possibilities, one for each stem, and each is one of the Five Elements in either yang (阳) or yin (阴) polarity.

The ten, in one pass

  • 甲 (jiǎ) — yang wood. The tall tree: upright, principled, slow to bend, grows in one direction. Thrives with room to grow; struggles when boxed in.
  • 乙 (yǐ) — yin wood. The vine or flowering grass: flexible, adaptive, finds a way around obstacles rather than through them.
  • 丙 (bǐng) — yang fire. The sun: warm, visible, generous with energy, at its best in the open. Dislikes being ignored more than being opposed.
  • 丁 (dīng) — yin fire. The lamp flame: focused, penetrating, quietly persistent. Reads people well; burns steadily rather than brightly.
  • 戊 (wù) — yang earth. The mountain: stable, protective, hard to move — a reliability that can shade into stubbornness.
  • 己 (jǐ) — yin earth. The field soil: nurturing, absorbent, cultivating. Works on people and projects patiently, from below.
  • 庚 (gēng) — yang metal. The axe or raw ore: decisive, dutiful, cuts through. Justice-minded; blunt when unpolished.
  • 辛 (xīn) — yin metal. The jewel: refined, precise, image-aware. Small, hard, and sharper than it looks.
  • 壬 (rén) — yang water. The river or sea: expansive, resourceful, always moving, hard to contain.
  • 癸 (guǐ) — yin water. The rain or dew: subtle, perceptive, seeps in everywhere; the quiet strategist of the ten.

These portraits are a doorway, not a verdict. Two 丙 fire people can live very different lives, because the Day Master is only the subject of the sentence — the rest of the chart supplies the verbs.

Strength changes everything

Classical technique first asks whether your Day Master is strong (身强) or weak (身弱): does the season of your birth month feed it, and do the other characters support or drain it? The answer flips the whole reading. A strong Day Master welcomes challenge and output (the elements that drain it become productive); a weak one needs support first (the elements that feed it become the medicine). This strength assessment is a rule-based computation — month-season weighting plus rooted support — not an intuition, and it is the foundation the next article builds on: your favourable elements, or 用神.
