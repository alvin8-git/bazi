How to Read a Four Pillars Chart: Stems, Branches, Hidden Stems and the Four Palaces

By BaziMaster 2026-09-19 Updated 2026-10-09

A BaZi chart (四柱八字) is a grid of eight characters. Each of the year, month, day and hour of birth gets a heavenly stem (天干) and an earthly branch (地支). The stem of the day pillar is the Day Master (日主), and every other character in the chart is read in relation to it.

What is in each of the four pillars?

Each pillar is a pair of characters. The stem sits on top and the branch sits underneath. The ten stems are 甲 乙 丙 丁 戊 己 庚 辛 壬 癸. The twelve branches are 子 丑 寅 卯 辰 巳 午 未 申 酉 戌 亥. Every stem and every branch belongs to one of the five elements, and each is yin or yang.

The sample person here is 王大明, a fictional man born on 1988-03-15 at 09:30 in Singapore. His pillars are 戊辰 (year), 乙卯 (month), 己巳 (day) and 戊辰 (hour). His Day Master is 己, yin Earth. Everything below uses this one chart, so you can follow each step without switching examples.

The stem is the visible, declared part of a pillar. The branch is its foundation. A branch also hides one to three further stems inside it, which the next section covers. The year branch carries the zodiac animal most people know their birth year by.

Key facts

  • A chart has 4 pillars and 8 characters, and each pillar is one stem plus one branch.
  • The sample chart is 戊辰 乙卯 己巳 戊辰, with Day Master 己 (yin Earth).
  • The four palaces cover ages 0-15 (year), 16-32 (month), 33-48 (day) and 49+ (hour).
  • Branch 辰 hides 戊, 乙 and 癸, branch 卯 hides 乙, and branch 巳 hides 丙, 庚 and 戊.
  • The engine rates the Day Master 身弱 (weak), with a score of -0.97 and roots in 辰, 巳 and 辰.
  • The clock time 09:30 becomes true solar time 08:16 before the pillars are cast.

What are hidden stems, and why do they matter?

Hidden stems (藏干) are the extra stems stored inside a branch. A chart that looks thin in one element on the surface may carry it below. This is why any serious element count includes them. The engine lists the hidden stems for all twelve branches in Table 2.

How do the four pillars become four palaces?

Classical doctrine reads the same four columns a second way, as the four palaces (宮位). The palace tells you which area of life a character colours and roughly which stage of life it speaks to. Table 3 gives the ages and the sample's reading.

How is the chart judged from the Day Master?

Once the pillars are cast, the first judgement is whether the Day Master is strong or weak. The engine uses a four-step method, shown here for the sample.

  1. Season (得令). The month branch 卯 is Wood, and Wood controls Earth, so the season state is 死 (月令剋我). This scores -2.0.
  2. Support. Peers (比劫) of Earth weigh 5.33 and resource (印) of Fire weighs 1.0, so support is 6.33.
  3. Drain. Output, wealth and authority combined weigh 3.67.
  4. Root. The Day Master is rooted (有根) in 辰, 巳 and 辰.

The result is 身弱, a weak Day Master, with a score of -0.97 in an ordinary (正格) structure. The engine then picks its favourable elements, Fire and Earth, using two of the four 用神 methods: 扶抑法 (support or drain) and 調候法 (seasonal climate). Strength is a measure of balance, not of ability. It only decides which elements act as medicine for this chart.

One caveat belongs here (method note). The engine reads the month by the branch's own element. The 旺相休囚死 school reads it by the season's element, and the two can differ in the days around a season change.

What changes for Singapore and Malaysia readers?

Three details decide whether your pillars match the sample's logic.

  • Year boundary. The BaZi year turns at 立春, not on 1 January or Chinese New Year. In 2027, 立春 falls on 2027-02-04. The engine casts 丙午 as the year pillar for 2027-01-20 and 丁未 for 2027-02-10. A Singapore or Malaysia birth in late January or early February needs a check against the exact moment.
  • Time zone. Singapore ran on UTC+7:30 before 1982-01-01 and UTC+8 after it. Malaysia has used UTC+8 since 1982-01-01. A birth certificate time before 1982 must be read with the older offset.
  • True solar time. The engine converts clock time to true solar time using longitude and the equation of time. The sample's 09:30 becomes 08:16, using longitude 103.8517°E and an equation of time of -9.4 minutes. A birth close to an hour boundary can change the hour pillar.

If you are unsure of the birth hour, run the chart with a few candidate times and see whether the hour pillar changes. If it does not, the doubt does not matter for the pillars.

FAQ

What are the four pillars in BaZi?

The four pillars are the year, month, day and hour of birth. Each gets a heavenly stem on top and an earthly branch underneath, making eight characters. The stem of the day pillar is the Day Master, and everything else is read in relation to it.

What are hidden stems in a BaZi chart?

Hidden stems (藏干) are extra stems stored inside a branch. A chart that looks thin in one element on the surface may carry it below. This is why a serious element count includes them. In the sample, branch 辰 hides 戊, 乙 and 癸.

What do the four palaces mean?

The palaces read the same four columns as areas of life. They cover ages 0-15 for the year, 16-32 for the month, 33-48 for the day and 49 onward for the hour. The ten gods say what an energy is, and the palace says where and when.

How do I know if my Day Master is strong or weak?

The engine checks the season of the month branch, then support, then drain, then roots. The result is a signed score. The sample chart scores -0.97, which is 身弱. Strength measures balance, not ability.

Which birth details matter for BaZi in Singapore or Malaysia?

The BaZi year turns at 立春, not 1 January, so late January or early February births need checking. Singapore ran on UTC+7:30 before 1982-01-01. The engine also converts clock time to true solar time, and the sample's 09:30 became 08:16.
