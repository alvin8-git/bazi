Symbolic Stars (神煞) in BaZi: What They Add

By BaziMaster 2026-09-19 Updated 2026-10-09

神煞 are symbolic stars: fixed lookup tables that attach a named star to a chart when particular stem and branch combinations occur. They are annotation on top of the structural reading. The element balance, ten gods and palaces carry the weight, and the stars add texture.

How do the lookup tables attach a star?

Each star has a key and a table. The key is a stem, a branch or an element taken from the chart. The table returns one or more branches. If any pillar's branch matches, the star is attached to that pillar. The pillar then decides the life area and era the star colours, as the four palaces describe.

The sample is 王大明, a fictional man born 1988-03-15 at 09:30 in Singapore. His pillars are 戊辰 乙卯 己巳 戊辰 and his Day Master is 己. The engine checks the stem-keyed nobles from both the day stem and the year stem. It checks the branch-keyed stars from both the year branch and the day branch.

Key facts

  • A star is a pure lookup: a key from the chart goes into a fixed table, and a matching branch attaches the star to that pillar.
  • For Day Master 己, the 文昌 branch is 酉, the 學堂 branch is 寅, and the 天乙貴人 branches are 子 and 申.
  • The sample chart carries three stars: 太極貴人 (year and hour), 華蓋 (year and hour) and 劫煞 (day).
  • The sample has no 天乙貴人, 文昌貴人, 學堂, 桃花, 驛馬, 祿神 or 空亡.
  • The sample's void branches are 戌 and 亥, taken from the day pillar 己巳, and none of his branches falls there.
  • In the engine's learning score, a 華蓋 adds +10 points of a 70-point result, so a star nudges the score and never decides it.

How much weight should the stars get?

Less than the structure, every time. A Nobleman does not rescue a chart whose useful element is buried, and a Void does not sink one whose structure is sound. The honest use of 神煞 is descriptive precision. They give a name to textures people recognise in themselves, such as the student who tests as brilliant but studies alone.

When a star and the structure agree, say it once with confidence. When they disagree, the structure wins. That ordering is how the engine builds every reading it computes. The sample's structure is a weak Day Master with 火 and 土 as favourable elements, and the three stars only colour it.

What changes for Singapore and Malaysia readers?

Stars depend on the year pillar, the day pillar and the month branch, so the local calendar rules matter.

  • Year boundary. The year stem and year branch change at 立春, which falls on 2027-02-04 for the 2027 year. A birth in late January or early February may carry the previous year's stem. That gives a different set of nobles and a different branch group.
  • Time zone. Singapore ran on UTC+7:30 before 1982-01-01 and UTC+8 after it. Malaysia has used UTC+8 since 1982-01-01. A wrong offset can change the hour pillar, and the hour pillar is one of the four places a star can land.
  • Month branch. 天德 and 月德 key off the month branch, which turns at the solar terms and not on the calendar month.
  • Homes. The 文昌 star is the one with a direct use in an HDB flat or condominium unit. It tells you which corner of the unit suits a desk. See the study corner guide.

Does a 天乙貴人 Nobleman star rescue a weak chart?

What if my chart has no 桃花 or 天乙貴人 star?

Why does my year of birth matter for stars in Singapore or Malaysia?
