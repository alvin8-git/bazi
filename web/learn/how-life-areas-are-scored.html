How the Six Life-Area Scores Are Built

By BaziMaster 2026-10-11 Updated 2026-10-11

Each reading scores six life areas out of 100: love and attraction, relationship stability, career, wealth, learning and health. Every score starts at 50 and moves by a short list of named rules, each tied to one classical idea, so you can read exactly why a number is what it is. A second, separate figure, the percentile, says where that score stands among other charts. It does not replace the score and it is not a measure of strength.

What are the six life areas?

Each area asks one question of the chart and reads a handful of Ten Gods (the ten roles that the other stems and branches play toward your Day Master), symbolic stars and palace checks. See the Ten Gods and Day Masters if these are new.

How is a score built?

A score is 50 plus the sum of its chips, kept between 5 and 95. A chip is one rule with one signed number, and the full list is printed under every score in the reading. A score of 70 or more reads prominent, 45 to 69 reads balanced, and under 45 reads needs support.

The thresholds in the chips (a star share of 15%, 20% and so on) and the sizes of the points are the engine's own calibration. What a star or a structure means is the classical part. The engine therefore labels the whole score a modern synthesis, and says so in the source line of every area.

Key facts

  • Six areas, each 50 plus named chips, clamped to 5 to 95. Bands: 70 and above prominent, 45 to 69 balanced, under 45 needs support.
  • Health is read from the Day Master's root, balance and the supply of the useful god. The organ map is not scored; it stays in the advice text.
  • The percentile is a second figure: where the score stands in a fixed reference sample of 10,000 fictional charts. It is a relative standing among charts, not strength.
  • The reference is frozen as area_reference_v1. A change to any rule means a new version, never a silent regeneration.
  • Classical books are cited at book level; a chapter is named only where it has been confirmed.

A worked example, using the fictional man 王大明 (born 1988-03-15, 09:30, weak Day Master). His learning score is 50, plus 10 for resource present, plus 10 for the canopy star, plus 5 for authority feeding resource, which makes 75: prominent. Every chip of that sum is listed in his reading.

Why is health read from the Day Master and not from an organ map?

An older version of the health score counted organ-map flags: eight points off for each element at 30% or more, or at 7% or less, of the chart. We removed that from the score for three reasons.

  1. The classics pair elements with organs, but they give no percentage cut. The 30% and 7% lines were the engine's, resting on its own hidden-stem weights. A 7% share is less than the weight of one stem, so a flag could appear or vanish on a single stem.
  2. The classics read illness where the useful god or the Day Master is damaged, not wherever shares are lopsided. A useful element in excess is not an affliction, and a harmful element that is missing is not one either.
  3. The classics read illness mostly through the luck cycles and the year. A natal score can only describe a tendency, which is why it is labelled constitution.

So health now reads three things the classics do read: the root of the Day Master, balance, and the supply of the useful god. The organ-map flags stay in the reading's advice text as a watch list of elements and the body systems that tradition pairs with them. They are reference for reflection, not medical advice, and they no longer move a number. The "For Me" health card follows the same rule, so the card and the reading agree.

What is the percentile, and what is it not?

The raw scores sit on different scales. A typical learning score is 90 and a typical stability score is 45, so a 60 means something different in each. The word "prominent" is easy to earn in learning and hard to earn in stability. To compare areas fairly, each score is also placed in a frozen reference sample of fictional charts.

The percentile is the share of reference charts that score below the number, with half of those that tie counted, as a whole number from 0 to 100. A learning score of 75 is prominent, yet its percentile is 19, because most reference charts score higher in learning. 王大明's health score of 67 is balanced, with a percentile of 90.

The percentile is a relative standing among charts, not strength. It does not make the score more or less true and it does not change a band. It is used where areas are compared with each other: the strongest and weakest area on your card and in the story, the priority line, and the lowest-area sentence for children. Every sentence that states a score still states the score.

What is in the reference sample?

The sample is 10,000 fictional charts, drawn with a fixed random seed (20261011). Birth dates are uniform across the days from 1930-01-01 to 2024-12-31, with a uniform minute of the day and an even split of sexes. It is version area_reference_v1, built against engine version life-areas-v2, and stored as a histogram of scores per area. Nobody's real birth data is in it.

FAQ

How is each life-area score calculated?

Each score starts at 50 and adds the chips listed under it in your reading, then is kept between 5 and 95. Each chip is one rule with one signed number, tied to a classical idea such as the spouse star, authority feeding resource, or the Day Master's root.

Why is my health score not based on organs?

Because the classics give no percentage cut for an organ flag, and they read illness through the Day Master and the useful god. The score reads the Day Master's root, balance and the supply of the useful god. The organ-map flags remain in the advice text but no longer move the score.

What does the percentile beside my score mean?

It is where your score stands among a fixed sample of 10,000 fictional charts, from 0 to 100. It is a relative standing among charts, not strength. A prominent score can have a low percentile when that area is easy to score high in.

Why is my strongest area not my highest score?

Because the strongest area on your card is picked by percentile, not by the raw number. Learning scores run high for most charts, so a learning 75 can stand lower among charts than a health 67.

Will the percentile change if the engine changes?

Not silently. The reference is frozen as area_reference_v1. If a rule changes, a new version is built next to it and the percentile names the version it used.

Sources and method

  • Ten Gods, stars and palaces: the classical Zi Ping structure readings, cited at book level in Zi Ping Zhen Quan, Di Tian Sui and San Ming Tong Hui.
  • Thresholds and point sizes: the engine's own calibration, labelled a modern synthesis. They are not classical numbers.
  • Health: the Day Master's root, balance and the supply of the useful god, as a static natal tendency. The organ map is reference and not medical advice.
  • Percentile: the mid-rank share inside area_reference_v1, built by scripts/build_area_reference.py with seed 20261011.
