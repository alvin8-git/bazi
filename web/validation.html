How the engine was validated

The method, a table you can re-run, and what none of it proves

Last updated: 2026-10-11

This site says its home engine reproduced a paid professional audit to the digit, with 27 of 27 flying-star palace numbers and 32 of 32 directional assignments. That is true. It was one home and one audit, and the audit is a private document that cannot be published. So this page shows what it can: what was compared, how a match was decided, the engine's full output for an invented home that you can re-run, and the same functions checked against examples that other people have published.

The short version

  • One home, one paid audit from 2013, one practice. This is a single comparison, not a benchmark suite.
  • You cannot check that audit yourself. You can check everything else on this page.
  • Section 4 prints the engine's 27 flying-star numbers and 32 Eight House assignments for an invented home, each with its rule. A test fails if the printed tables ever differ from a fresh run of the engine.
  • Section 5 checks the same arithmetic against published sources. All 648 numbers in 24 published flying-star charts agree, and a published Eight House verse agrees in 56 of 56 places in one printing and 54 of 56 in another.

1. What was compared

The site's author bought a written feng shui audit of one residence from a professional practice in 2013, and compared the engine's output with it line by line. The totals were 27 flying-star numbers and 32 Eight House assignments. This page counts them as follows, and section 4 uses the same count.

  • A nine-palace flying-star chart has three numbers in each palace: the period star, the mountain star and the facing star. Nine palaces times three numbers is 27.
  • Eight House gives one star to each of the eight directions for a person's life trigram. Eight directions for each of four people is 32.

A match means the engine printed the same number, or the same star name, as the audit for the same palace or direction. There was no tolerance, no rounding and no partial credit. One different digit would have been a miss.

Not published: the name of the practice, the practitioner, the property and everyone connected with it, and any page, quote or image of the document. It is a third party's paid work about a private home.

2. How the engine produces each number

Each number comes from one engine function and one rule. Periods are 20 years long and count from 1864, so period 8 runs from 2004 to 2023.

NumberEngine functionRule
Period starbase_chartThe period number goes in the centre and flies forward along the Luoshu path: centre, northwest, west, northeast, south, north, southwest, east, southeast.
Mountain starnatal_chartThe period star in the sitting palace goes in the centre and flies forward or backward. The direction comes from the mountain, in the same yuan as the sitting mountain, in that star's own trigram.
Facing starnatal_chartThe same rule, starting from the period star in the facing palace.
Which chartnatal_chart_from_degreesThe compass bearing gives the facing mountain, one of 24 of 15 degrees each. Within 3 degrees of its centre the plain chart is used. From 3 to 6.5 degrees the replacement-star chart is used. Within 1 degree of a boundary both neighbouring charts are shown.
Life trigramming_guaAdd the digits of the birth year, counted from Start of Spring, until one digit is left. Men take 11 minus that digit, women take that digit plus 4. A result of 5 becomes 2 for men and 8 for women.

3. What this does not show

  • One home. A single residence tests a small part of the possible charts.
  • One practice. The audit is one professional's work. Practitioners differ, and agreeing with one is not agreeing with all.
  • One document. Nobody outside can repeat the comparison, because nobody else can see the document.
  • Agreement is not proof. Two sources that print the same number show that they apply the same convention. They do not show that the convention describes anything real. Schools disagree: some lineages chart a mixed facing that stays inside one trigram with the plain chart, where this engine switches to the replacement-star chart beyond 3 degrees.
  • Arithmetic only. Matching star numbers and direction names says nothing about whether the readings built on them are right.
  • Not the calendar layer. The separate claim that BaZi charts match commercial chart products is not covered here.

4.4 Re-run it

The tables above are written by a script that calls the engine functions named in section 2. A test fails if this page and a fresh run ever differ. The script ships with the engine's source code.

5.2 The Eight House verse

The direction stars are taught as a verse, the Great Wandering Year song, with one line for each life trigram. A line names the stars of the other seven trigrams, going clockwise from north and skipping its own. One source spells this out for one line, and the table reads it out. We read every line the same way.

5.3 One published sentence

The Wikipedia article on Flying Star Feng Shui says: "In a Period-8 building that faces southwest, the number that locates in Facing Palace is number 5 whereas the number in Sitting Palace is number 2." The engine's period 8 base chart puts 5 in the southwest palace and 2 in the northeast palace, the palace opposite. Both numbers agree.
