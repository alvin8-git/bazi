Questions people ask first

Is bazifor.me free?

Yes. The BaZi reading, the home feng shui audit, the Kua number calculator and the baby name builder are free, with no account and no sign-up.

Do I need an account to keep my results?

No. Your workspace lives behind an unguessable private link. Keep the link to come back to it; nothing is listed or shared.

Is the reading written by AI?

No. Every line is the output of a fixed classical rule, and the guides are hand-written. No generative AI writes any reading, and the same inputs always give the same result.

Does it predict events?

No. The engine computes classical structures such as the four pillars, the useful elements and the Flying Stars of a home, and states their traditional reading. It does not forecast events.

Why is it built for Singapore and Malaysia?

Birth times are converted to true solar time for the longitude, and clock times before 1982 use the UTC+7:30 offset of that era. The home tool is written for HDB flats and condominiums, and every term appears in English with the Chinese once.
