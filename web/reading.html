How to read this

Five tabs, five questions. Start at the top and move right.

  • Pillars (四柱 sì zhù): what you are made of, from your birth moment, plus your Day Master (日主 rì zhǔ).
  • Elements (五行 wǔ xíng): which element is heavy or thin, and which one balances the chart.
  • Gods (十神 shí shén): the ten roles the other stems play toward you, and what drives you.
  • Timing (大運 dà yùn): the luck cycles, this year, and the next few windows. Navigation notes, not predictions.
  • Compass (命卦 mìng guà): your Kua number and the directions to face, sleep and work.

Scores and labels are engine output. The classical rule behind each line is cited beside it.
