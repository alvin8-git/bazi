About bazifor.me关于我们

bazifor.me began with a family question and an engineer's irritation. The question: which bedroom should each child sleep in, which way should the beds point, when should we move? The irritation: every answer available was either a paid consultation you had to take on faith, or an AI chatbot that produced different answers each time you asked.

The classical literature — 八字 four pillars, 八宅 Eight House, 玄空飞星 Flying Stars, 择日 date selection — turns out to be far more procedural than its mystique suggests. Rules, tables, precedence orders. So we did what engineers do: encoded the procedures, tested them against benchmarks, and made every output cite its source.

What we believe三条原则

1 · Computation before interpretation. The calendar math has one right answer; get it exactly right (立春 boundaries, true solar time, historical clock regimes) before saying anything about a life.

2 · Every claim carries its citation. A reading you cannot check is a performance. Each line of a bazifor.me reading names the classical rule that produced it — so you can look the rule up, disagree with the school, or verify the arithmetic.

3 · No AI guesswork at reading time. No large language model generates your results. The engine is deterministic: same input, same output, forever — which is what makes validation possible at all.

The benchmark test: we reproduced a professionally produced, paid fengshui audit to the digit — 27/27 flying-star palaces, 32/32 directional assignments — and match commercial chart products character-for-character on the calendar layer. That standard is only meetable by a system that computes.

Who it's for为谁而做

Families making real decisions: choosing between homes, allocating bedrooms, pointing beds and desks, naming a baby, picking a renovation window or a moving date. The tools handle up to eight people and six homes in one private workspace, in English and 简体中文, with Singapore-grade calendar handling (yes, including the pre-1982 UTC+7:30 clock).
