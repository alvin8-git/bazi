      What is a Kua number?

      The Kua number (命卦, "life trigram") is the Eight Mansions (八宅) label for a person. It is one of eight trigrams, numbered 1 to 9 with 5 skipped, taken from the birth year counted from 立春 and from the person's sex. Each trigram belongs to the East group (東四命: 坎 1, 震 3, 巽 4, 離 9) or the West group (西四命: 坤 2, 乾 6, 兌 7, 艮 8).

      The trigram fixes eight directions around you: four favourable (生氣 Sheng Qi, 天醫 Tian Yi, 延年 Yan Nian, 伏位 Fu Wei) and four to avoid (禍害 Huo Hai, 六煞 Liu Sha, 五鬼 Wu Gui, 絕命 Jue Ming), by the 八宅大遊年 line-change rule. The calculator applies the same table the home audit uses, so the two always agree.

      Worked example and lookup table

      A fictional man born 15 March 1988 in Singapore: the year 1988 counts in full because 立春 fell on 4 February 1988. Its digits add to 26, then to 8; for a man the number is 11 minus 8, so his Kua is 3, the trigram 震 Zhen, East group. His best headboard directions are south (生氣 Sheng Qi), north (天醫 Tian Yi) and south-east (延年 Yan Nian). The number 5 is never assigned: a man who computes 5 takes 2 (坤) and a woman takes 8 (艮).

In Singapore and Malaysia an HDB flat or condominium bedroom usually offers only two solid walls, so pick the better of the two available directions rather than the best on paper.
