      What is a Kua number?

      The idea behind the calculator, in two paragraphs and a table.

      The Kua number ("life trigram") is the Eight Mansions (八宅) label for a person. It is one of eight trigrams, numbered 1 to 9 with 5 skipped, taken from the birth year counted from Start of Spring and from the person's sex. Each trigram belongs to the East group (東四命: 坎 (north) 1, 震 (east) 3, 巽 (south-east) 4, 離 (south) 9) or the West group (西四命: 坤 (south-west) 2, 乾 (north-west) 6, 兌 (west) 7, 艮 (north-east) 8).

      The trigram fixes eight directions around you: four favourable (Vitality (生氣), Heavenly Doctor (天醫), Longevity (延年), Stability (伏位)) and four to avoid (Mishap (禍害), Six Killings (六煞), Five Ghosts (五鬼), Severed Fate (絕命)), by the Eight Mansions wandering-year line-change rule (八宅大遊年). The calculator applies the same table the home audit uses, so the two always agree.

      Worked example and lookup table

      Check the result by hand.

      A fictional man born 15 March 1988 in Singapore: the year 1988 counts in full because Start of Spring fell on 4 February 1988. Its digits add to 26, then to 8; for a man the number is 11 minus 8, so his Kua is 3, the trigram 震 (east), East group. His best headboard directions are south (Vitality), north (Heavenly Doctor) and south-east (Longevity). The number 5 is never assigned: a man who computes 5 takes 2 (坤) and a woman takes 8 (艮).
