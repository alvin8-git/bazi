Annotate plan

Facing 0° N (坎, north)

Facing bearing: stand at the main door looking out and read the phone compass in degrees. Rooms: draw each room as a box, name it, and mark where beds are.

Trace every enclosed area once, rooms and the spaces between them; balconies stay out of the centre. Measure the facing with a phone compass at the main window/balcony, away from metal. Drag on the plan to draw each room; tap 🚪 then click the main door position.
How to trace
  1. Trace all enclosed floor area, corridors, lobby, yard, WC, shelter, walk-in wardrobe. The centre point is the balance point of the whole home, so a missing area shifts every palace.
  2. No overlaps, no gaps, each patch of floor belongs to exactly one shape; walls are nothing.
  3. Balconies and patios are drawn but excluded from the centre (dashed), open to the sky and outside the envelope you live in; they still get a palace reading for the water star (向星) and the gate check.
  4. Furniture inside a room is not a room, a wardrobe, bay platform or bed stays inside its bedroom's shape; only a walk-in with its own walls and door is traced as Wardrobe.
  5. One child per bedroom, parents in the master, set Beds on each bedroom; the optimiser never exceeds it.
