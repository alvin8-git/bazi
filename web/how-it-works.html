How it works (运作原理)

Most "AI fortune telling" sites feed your birthday to a chatbot and hope the prose sounds wise. bazifor.me does the opposite: everything is computed by a rules engine — the same classical procedures a careful practitioner runs by hand, encoded and tested. No large language model touches your reading. Here is the pipeline, step by step.

1You enter birth details. Date, time, and sex — for yourself, or a family of up to 8. No account, no email: your workspace lives behind an unguessable private link that only you hold. Unsure of the birth time? Mark it, and time-dependent sections are flagged rather than faked.
2The engine casts the chart — exactly. Four pillars with the Start of Spring (立春) year boundary, solar-term months, the unbroken 60-day Jia Zi (甲子) cycle, and true solar time correction (including historical clock regimes — a pre-1982 Singapore birth is adjusted for the old UTC+7:30 standard). Then the derived layers: Day Master (日主) strength, useful god (用神) favourable elements, Ten Gods (十神), luck cycles (大运), Eight House kua number (命卦) and direction maps, Wenchang (文昌) study positions.
3The reading assembles itself from cited rules. Each section shows three things: the computed data, a plain-language interpretation (解读) narrative, and a strategy — and every line names the classical rule that produced it. You can disagree with a rule; you can never be left wondering where a claim came from.

Clock time in, true solar time out. The engine takes the clock time and the birth city, finds that city's offset from UTC on that date (daylight saving and past changes included, such as Singapore's UTC+7:30 before 1982), then converts to true solar time using the city's longitude and the equation of time: 09:30 on the clock in Singapore becomes 08:16 true solar time at 103.85°E. The time zone alone is not solar time; longitude is what moves the sun. The BaZi year changes at Start of Spring, not on 1 January; Start of Spring 2027 falls on 2027-02-04.

The home fengshui pipeline (宅运分析)

1Upload a floor plan and set the facing. Any photo or PDF page of your plan; enter the compass facing you measured at the main window. If your facing sits near a chart boundary (mixed facing, 兼向 / straddling a boundary, 骑线), the engine computes both candidate charts and says so, instead of silently picking one.
2Trace rooms — and optionally doors and windows. Draw each room over the plan; zoom in to mark where doors and windows sit. Doors unlock the form-school audit: entry-line checks (door facing bed, 门冲床), commanding position, door-facing-door pairs.
3Analyze. One click computes the Flying Star chart for your building's period and facing, scores every person against every bedroom (three layers: Eight House + Flying Stars + useful god, each contribution cited), proposes bed and desk placement wall by wall, generates room colour guidance, a renovation-timing table against this year's and next year's afflictions, and a move-in date finder (入宅) for your household. Add up to six homes and compare them side by side.
