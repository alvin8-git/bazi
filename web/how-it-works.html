How it works运作原理

Most "AI fortune telling" sites feed your birthday to a chatbot and hope the prose sounds wise. bazifor.me does the opposite: everything is computed by a rules engine — the same classical procedures a careful practitioner runs by hand, encoded and tested. No large language model touches your reading. Here is the pipeline, step by step.

1You enter birth details. Date, time, and sex — for yourself, or a family of up to 8. No account, no email: your workspace lives behind an unguessable private link that only you hold. Unsure of the birth time? Mark it, and time-dependent sections are flagged rather than faked.
2The engine casts the chart — exactly. Four pillars with the 立春 year boundary, solar-term months, the unbroken 60-day 甲子 cycle, and true solar time correction (including historical clock regimes — a pre-1982 Singapore birth is adjusted for the old UTC+7:30 standard). Then the derived layers: Day Master strength, 用神 favourable elements, Ten Gods, luck cycles 大运, Eight House 命卦 and direction maps, 文昌 study positions.
3The reading assembles itself from cited rules. Each section shows three things: the computed data, a plain-language 解读 narrative, and a strategy — and every line names the classical rule that produced it. You can disagree with a rule; you can never be left wondering where a claim came from.

The home fengshui pipeline宅运分析

1Upload a floor plan and set the facing. Any photo or PDF page of your plan; enter the compass facing you measured at the main window. If your facing sits near a chart boundary (兼向/骑线), the engine computes both candidate charts and says so, instead of silently picking one.
2Trace rooms — and optionally doors and windows. Draw each room over the plan; zoom in to mark where doors and windows sit. Doors unlock the form-school audit: entry-line checks (门冲床), commanding position, door-facing-door pairs.
3Analyze. One click computes the Flying Star chart for your building's period and facing, scores every person against every bedroom (three layers: 八宅 + 玄空 + 用神, each contribution cited), proposes bed and desk placement wall by wall, generates room colour guidance, a renovation-timing table against this year's and next year's afflictions, and an 入宅 move-in date finder for your household. Add up to six homes and compare them side by side.
