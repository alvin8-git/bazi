Flying stars for 2027 (玄空飛星)

The annual nine-palace grid, and how the engine scores each star

In a flying-star year the nine stars are dealt again across the centre and the eight compass palaces. The deal depends on the year only, not on any building. This page prints the 2027 deal as the bazifor.me engine computes it, the rule that produces it, and the score the engine gives each star in homes of each period.

In 2027, star 9 is in the centre. The 5 Yellow is in the north, where the engine scores it −2.0 in homes of most periods, the lowest score it gives any star.

How the engine scores a star

The engine scores a star by its relation to the period of the home. Periods are 20 years long and count from 1864, so period 8 runs from 2004 to 2023 and period 9 from 2024 to 2043. A home built in period 8 is a period 8 home, and so on. The conditions below are checked from the top, and the first one that fits gives the score.

How the page stays true to the engine

The grid comes from the engine function that the home reports call for the annual chart, and the scores come from the function that scores a star. A test regenerates this page from those functions.

The test fails if a single cell differs.

What this page is not

  • Not a reading of your home. The grid is the same for every building. Which room sits in which palace depends on the home, and that is what the home analyzer works out.
  • Not a classical score. Classical texts call stars prosperous, rising, retreating or troublesome. They do not give numbers. The scores are the engine's own weights, and the engine cites no source for the numbers.
  • Not a forecast. A score ranks a star against the period of a home. It does not predict what happens in a room.
  • Not the other annual markers. The Grand Duke, the Year Breaker and the Three Killings are covered in the 2027 guide, not here.
