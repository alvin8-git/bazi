Flying stars (玄空飛星) are a feng shui method that places nine numbered stars across the centre and eight compass sectors of a building, and re-deals the annual stars every year at Start of Spring (立春). In 2027, a 丁未 (Fire Goat) year, star 9 enters the centre and the 5 Yellow star 5 sits in the north.

## What does the 2027 annual chart look like?

The annual chart is the same for every building on earth: it depends only on the year, not on your address. What differs is which of your rooms falls in which sector. The engine that powers our home readings gives this distribution for 2027.

## When does the flying-star year change?

At Start of Spring, 2027-02-04, not on 1 January and not at Chinese New Year. A condo owner who finishes a renovation on 2 February 2027 is still working in the 丙午 (Fire Horse) year, with a different chart. The year pillar shows the switch: a date of 2027-01-20 carries 丙午, and 2027-02-10 carries 丁未.

The centre star moves one step each year. In 2026 the centre star is 1, in 2027 it is 9 and in 2028 it is 8. The 5 Yellow therefore travels: south (離) in 2026, north (坎) in 2027 and southwest (坤) in 2028. Anything you plan across the 4 February boundary should be checked against both charts.

## Which annual afflictions apply in 2027?

Three year-spirits (year spirits, 年神) rotate with the year branch, and they are separate from the numbered stars. These positions are derived from the year spirits rules in 協紀辨方書 (Qing almanac manual) (method note: the engine's public output is the star chart, and these three are derived from the rules for the 丁未 year).

- Grand Duke sits on the year branch. For 未 that is southwest, the arc SW1.

- Year Breaker sits on the branch opposite the year branch. Opposite 未 is 丑, northeast, the arc NE1.

- Three Killings for a 亥 (Pig, Water) 卯 (Rabbit, Wood) 未 year sit on the opposite frame: the west, 申 酉 戌. That is SW3, W2 and NW1 in the 24-mountain ring.

Together with the north 5 Yellow, the sectors to treat carefully in 2027 are southwest, northeast, the western arc and north. For the 5 Yellow sector itself the engine's remedy table gives the traditional answer: place metal there (brass, a six-coin string or a metal pendulum clock) to drain the earth star, and keep it quiet (method note: engine remedy table). The practical rule is the traditional one: avoid structural work that breaks the ground or the wall (groundbreaking, 動土) in those sectors during the year. Assembling flat-pack furniture is not groundbreaking; hacking tiles, hacking walls and drilling for new points is.

## How do I read the chart for an HDB flat or condo?

Use the unit plan, not the block. Stand inside the unit at the main door looking out; that direction is the facing. Then overlay the nine-palace grid on the floor plan of your own unit, with north at the real compass north, and see which rooms fall in which palace. Neighbouring units in the same block face the same way but have different room layouts, so their palace map differs.

Two Singapore and Malaysia points matter in practice. First, a single flat can put its rooms in three or four different palaces, so the annual chart is read room by room and not for the flat as a whole. Second, a condo unit with a glass balcony wall has a clear facing but a looser boundary, so the room outline you trace in the tool decides which palace the balcony belongs to.

## What does a worked example show?

Take the fictional 王大明, born 1988-03-15, kua 震, East group. His Eight House map says his Vitality (生氣) direction is south (離), his Heavenly Doctor (天醫) is north (坎), his Longevity (延年) is southeast (巽), and east (震) is Stability (伏位), the stable seat.

Now overlay 2027:

- South, his best direction, carries the annual 4, a neutral star at 0.0. The sector stays usable and loses nothing; it just gains no annual boost.

- North, his Heavenly Doctor direction, carries 5 Yellow at -2.0. A study or bed that relies on that wall is working against the annual star for this year. The natal and personal layers still count, but the year layer is the heaviest single annual penalty.

- East, his Stability seat, carries the neutral 7.

- Southwest holds both the Grand Duke and the annual 6, a favourable auspicious white star at +0.5. The Grand Duke is the more specific warning for ground-breaking, so the rule is to keep it free of structural work even though the star is kind.

The conclusion is not "move out" or "avoid north". It is the overlay: in 2027, keep construction away from north, northeast, southwest and the west frame, and check the sleeping direction against the form rules in the bed direction guide.

## Why are annual stars a layer and not the whole story?

The annual chart sits on top of the building's natal chart, which comes from its facing and construction period under 沈氏玄空學 (Flying Stars classic), including the replacement chart (替卦) replacement stars used for mixed facing (兼向) facings. It also sits above your personal Eight House directions from Eight House (八宅). A sector can be natal-strong and annually afflicted, or the reverse. The reading that matters is the overlay of all three, and each layer should show the rule that produced it.
